Ours — mobile, round 1
390 × 844 · clickable
Try it
Sign in → Continue → type 123456 (anything else shows the error). Tabs switch screens. Tap any memory card, the sealed note, a calendar day or agenda row, the + button, and send a chat message. "Lock now" in Us shows the expired-session state.
Tweaks
Theme (light / late night), her name, and 3 timeline layouts: Editorial (mixed card sizes, per brief), Photo grid (dense, media-first), Journal (date-led, text-forward).
Design decisions
Terracotta is the one accent; sage is secondary and marks her events. Serif for titles and dates, sans for UI, mono for timestamps and eyebrows. Photos bleed edge-to-edge on the large card. "Her take" is a separate section, never merged. Placeholder photos are warmed and slightly desaturated.
Round 5 — the remainder
Gallery (grid icon on Memories): month-grouped grid with sticky headers, filters, Select → multi-select with the action bar → Delete confirm; Albums tab with Favorites, custom and one-per-memory. Full composer: "Keep writing" from the sheet, or "Start the memory" on the post-trip banner opens the from-plan prefilled state (day headings, item lists, filtered picker). Notes composer: "Leave a note" — color, schedule, seal; it lands on the board. Plan map under More: city chips, pins by kind, tap for a card. Us: currency, timezone, notifications with per-plan checks, and the two opt-in switches.
Round 4 — Plans, part 2
New tweak Simulate date: "in Tokyo" pins Japan at the top of Plans with a Today treatment and opens it in Today mode (local time, timeline with the next item highlighted, large confirmation codes, offline banner — tap it to toggle, Tomorrow button). "Just home" shows the post-trip banner on the Overview. "+ Add to day" opens the kind picker → full Flight and Stay forms, or Paste a confirmation (review state with detected fields). Open "Anniversary weekend" for the event variant: Schedule instead of Itinerary, Guests instead of Checklists.
Round 3 — Plans
New Plans tab (Memories · Plans · Calendar · Chat · Us). Plans list with Up next / Dreaming / Past; Japan, spring 2027 as the large card. Plan detail: Overview, Itinerary (unscheduled tray, 4-item day, 1-item day, empty day), Ideas board with two-avatar votes and filters, Bookings (flight, ryokan, Shinkansen, teamLab), and via More: Checklists (tick them), Budget (USD over JPY), Documents, Locked note (tap to reveal). Notes now live in Chat as a second segment. Lake weekend shows as a plan bar on the calendar; the Asheville memory carries a "Planned in" strip. Still to do on mobile: Today mode, item forms, paste-a-confirmation, event variant, post-trip banner, map.
Round 2
Display font is now a tweak — Newsreader (default), Cormorant Garamond, Lora, or the original Instrument Serif. Sign in follows the new brief (passkey button + Remember me on step 1, recovery-code link on step 2). Tap a gallery tile in a memory to open the lightbox; "Share to chat" drops it into the conversation.
